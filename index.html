A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before we
begin.

Can you create a whole children’s book?+

Yes. I offer complete end-to-end children’s book creation: illustrations, interior layout, front/back/spine cover design, eBook formatting, KDP and IngramSpark publishing support, and promotional videos. You can get everything done in one place.

How do you keep a character consistent?+

First, I study your story, characters, and preferred style. I design the characters for your approval before starting the complete illustration process, keeping 100% consistency from the first page to the last.

Do you use AI in your work?+

AI tools can be part of the creative process. Tell me your preferences at the start so the workflow and final deliverables match your brief. New artwork on this site is labelled as AI-assisted studio concepts.

How much will my project cost?+

Cost depends on illustration count, complexity, format, and timeline. Share your brief for a tailored quote or check current Fiverr packages.

Do you offer unlimited revisions?+

Yes. I offer unlimited revisions so we can refine the illustrations and design until you’re happy with the result.

Can you help me publish on KDP or IngramSpark?+

Yes. I provide complete publishing guidance, including assistance with uploading and publishing your book on Amazon KDP and IngramSpark. I also prepare high-quality print-ready files and professionally formatted eBooks.

Can you help promote my book?+

Yes. I create engaging book trailers and promotional video ads to help introduce your story and market your book.

Can I see every client review?+

Yes. Visit my Fiverr profile ↗ for the full public review history and current packages.
